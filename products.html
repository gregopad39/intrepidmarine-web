Product guide

A family of apps with one attitude: document the real work, keep proof of weather and progress, and hand off cleanly — whether you’re the owner, the yard, or the tech.

App Store / Play Store buttons will go here once listings are finalized. Until then, this page is the pitch deck.

Intrepid Mariner

Who it’s for: owners, skippers, and the friend who always gets called to “just take a look.”

Captain’s log with marine conditions from a local station (not random phone GPS weather), NEXRAD radar with a navigation emphasis mode, maintenance and checklists, friend’s boat consults, and exports a yard can actually use — PDF for people, .msworkorder for Marine Service.

Add screenshots later Suggested: radar with weather button, captain’s log weather panel, yard handoff share sheet.

Platforms: iOS · Android · App Clip / companion pieces as available.

CRS

Who it’s for: boat owners drowning in spare parts, garage entrepreneurs, and anyone whose inventory lives in three sheds and a tote labeled “misc.”

Locations, photos, valuations, garage-sale workflows, and the same practical radar map when weather decides whether the driveway sale happens. Transfer packages keep your catalog mobile.

Add screenshots later Suggested: inventory list, garage-sale board, radar tab.

Marine Service

Who it’s for: marina techs, mobile marine mechanics, small yards that need work orders without an enterprise ERP.

Stepped job entry (header → schedule → boat → notes), progress logs with photos, estimates, parts signals, and peer-to-peer .msworkorder sharing. Import handoffs from Intrepid Mariner so the owner’s problem statement doesn’t get re-typed wrong at the counter.

Add screenshots later Suggested: stepped new-job panel, job progress timeline, import success.

Service Tech + Service Valet brand

Who it’s for: land trades and the global gig economy — electricians by day, musicians by night, concrete crews, hospitality staff, tutors, mobile beauty, delivery helpers.

Firm profile with industries and services, industry-standard checklists (check in with the producer / foreman / banquet lead), stepped work-order entry, and documentation that protects communication and pay. Same soul as the marine tools — less salt, more drywall dust.

Add screenshots later Suggested: firm trades profile, trade workflow checklist, schedule board.

How the family fits together

  1. Owner documents the issue in Intrepid Mariner (photos, weather, notes).
  2. Handoff goes out as PDF and/or MST work-order package.
  3. Yard / tech imports into Marine Service and continues the job.
  4. Land crews use Service Tech when the same workflow leaves the water.
  5. Stuff & surplus lives in CRS when inventory is the problem.
