Intrepid Mariner

The skipper’s second brain — live weather, nested inventory, a log that remembers, and paperwork that looks like you run a shipyard, not a junk drawer.

Texas-built On-device No account Commercial marine
Get on the App Store Google Play Ask about a fleet

Who it’s for

Owners, skippers, and the person who always gets called to “just take a look.” Commercial operators who need proof of weather, gear, and work without standing up a back-office stack. iPhone, iPad, and Mac. No cloud account.

What it does

  • See what’s coming. Animated NWS NEXRAD. Weather loop, watches and warnings, NOAA depth and bathymetry in USN mode. Wind, seas, tides, forecast from the map. U.S. and greater North America public feeds — not worldwide, not a plotter, not primary navigation.
  • Find the part. Inventory nested the way a boat is stowed. Photos, quantities, loans and borrows, expenses. Vessel name, MMSI, HIN, and photos on the reports you hand to people who bill by the hour.
  • Write it like a captain. Journal, GPS track while the app is open, engine hours, guests, dictation, pre-departure checklists.
  • Fix it and prove it. Maintenance with a photo timeline. Schedules. Surveys — condition, insurance, in-water or hauled — with ratings, dictation, signature, signed PDF. Insurance claims with a trail instead of a guess.
  • Hand it to the yard. PDF for humans. Work-order package for Marine Service Tech so the owner’s problem statement is not re-typed wrong at the counter.

Premium is paperwork, not a paywall on the boat

Radar, inventory, log, checklists, maintenance, surveys, Customer Boat — all free. Premium unlocks backup and export only: inventory grids, PDF, CSV, full backup over AirDrop or Files.

Your boat. Your device.

We do not harvest lockers. Camera and microphone only when you take a picture or talk to the phone. Optional experimental NMEA over a local IP you enter. Not for primary navigation.
