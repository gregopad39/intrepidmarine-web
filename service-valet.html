Who it’s for

Independents and small shops who drive to the work: electrical, plumbing, HVAC, paint, roofing, carpentry, appliances, hospitality crews, events, and the rest of the trades that live on appointments. You do not need a back office to look professional. You need a ticket that says who, what, where, and when.

What you get

  • Jobs queue by phase — created, in progress, done, archived
  • Schedule board: who, what, where, half-hour appointments
  • Work order types that match the street: service, estimate, emergency, warranty, insurance, deposit, cancel, referral, handoff
  • Estimate visits that stay lean — walk the property, shoot the problem, publish. Not a fake “complete” gate
  • Problem → identification → testing with dictation when hands are dirty
  • Photos on the job. Cost lines for parts, labor, expenses
  • Tools inventory for the van and the shop; loan/borrow that comes back
  • Share .work-order packages by Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or Files

Your data stays with you

No account required for core field use. Tickets live on the device until you share a file. We are not a lead mill and we do not sell your customers.

Why it sits next to Intrepid Mariner

Marine commercial work taught us that documentation is how you get paid and how you sleep. Service Valet is that lesson on land. Same company. Same stubborn idea: the job is not done until the proof exists.

First 12 work orders are free per device. Subscription unlocks unlimited creates — list price is in the store.
