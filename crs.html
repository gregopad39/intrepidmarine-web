My Stuff

Photograph it. Dictate the note. Drop it in a location. Search later — jewelry, tools, the holiday bin, the charger that migrates.

Garage Sale

Multiple sales. Three photos an item. Firm or negotiable. PDF flyer. AirDrop a sale file to another iPhone when someone is on the driveway.

Field Reports

Accident, defect, property, injury. Photos and dictation. Close the case. Export a PDF with the pictures still in it — insurer, landlord, shop.

Weather

Animated NWS NEXRAD and warnings — United States only. Useful when Saturday’s sale meets a lying sky.

Borrow · Loan · Return

Who has the impact driver. Who borrowed the casserole. When it was supposed to come home. Photo optional.

Locations

Garage inside the house, bin inside the garage, jewelry box inside the bin. Nested the way a house is actually stowed.

Who it’s for

People who own too much stuff and a brain that will not file it. House, garage, cabin, car trunk, the vacation place. Photograph what’s in the bin. Dictate the note with dirty hands. Find it later without tearing the garage apart at 10 p.m.

What stays free

Inventory, locations, garage sales, field reports, borrow/loan, letter templates, and weather are free. Premium is backup and export only: PDF, CSV, full backup over AirDrop or Files. $4.99/month or $39/year — list price is in the store.

Your junk. Your device.

No ads. No account. Camera and microphone only when you take a picture or talk to the phone. We do not collect a dossier on your sock drawer.
